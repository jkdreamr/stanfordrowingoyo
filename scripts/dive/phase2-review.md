# Dive Phase 2 review

151 added constrained prompts; 210 total. Cutover: **2026-11-10**.

Schedule: **64 days, 210 constrained, 110 broad**, five unique themes and nonempty clusters/day, at least three constrained prompts/day, no repeated IDs in the cycle, and mixed set sizes. All constrained prompts are used. Largest constrained cluster: countries, **21/210 (10%)**. No added Pokémon or country/state letter predicates.

Broad prompts: **0 additional retirements** (the 11 Phase 1 overlap retirements and 32 total inactive/loadable prompts remain). Broad data is unchanged; historical dates retain the exact frozen 330-prompt/66-day selector.

## Dropped ideas and explicit scope decisions

- **French AOP/PDO cheeses:** dropped; the pinned comparison table mixes designation systems and did not yield a reliably complete set matching the claimed French AOP scope. No partial cheese set shipped.
- **Paris 2024 Olympic sports:** dropped because its complete programme includes rowing, prohibited by the general-interest bank brief. Replaced with the complete Milano Cortina 2026 Winter Olympic discipline programme.
- **Unavailable standalone continent-capital list pages:** replaced, not guessed; capital prompts use pinned sovereign-state tables plus individually pinned pages for de facto/economic seats. Multiple/disputed capitals are accepted generously.
- **Unawarded Bake Off season:** TBA is not a winner. The completed-season scope excludes that placeholder.
- **Penguin taxonomy:** the complete traditional 18-species grouping is explicit; common newer split-species names are accepted as aliases of their traditional group.
- **Tour de France:** stripped winners count, including both Lance Armstrong and Floyd Landis. War/pandemic cancellation labels are never answers.
- **US Supreme Court since 1981:** first appointments only; Rehnquist’s 1986 elevation is excluded, while Roberts and Scalia remain.
- **Historical Emmy categories:** predecessor categories count explicitly; date splits use award years.
- **Protected and disputed geography:** partially recognised countries are accepted; capital answers include current official and common de facto/economic seats. The national-park pin lists 64 parks, including Chiricahua.
