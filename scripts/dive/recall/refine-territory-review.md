# Integrated territory-consistency review

Rule: a country-with-X predicate uses the country's own metropolitan/integral territory, not its dependencies, unless the visible question explicitly says otherwise. Integral islands/exclaves are not automatically dependencies. Review date: 9 November 2026. Overlap is informational only.

## Changes

- Mediterranean membership: **24 → 23**. Remove United Kingdom and its four canonical/alias forms. France remains via mainland/Corsica. The original batch-2 raw and selected brainstorms have **no UK guess**, so no historical guess was relabelled: four explicit, source-backed negative fixtures were added in `refine-territories-selected.json` and appended by the evaluator.
- France borders: the pre-existing acceptance note already allowed overseas/disputed borders, but the visible question did not. Make the overseas exception visible; retain all members.
- Island countries: acceptance notes erroneously said Australia counts while the pinned source and 50-member data exclude it as continental. Correct the notes, not the answer list.

## Independent status evidence opened

- [UK Government, Overseas Territories](https://www.gov.uk/types-of-british-nationality/british-overseas-territories-citizen): Gibraltar and the sovereign-base areas are separate overseas territories; UK citizenship does not make them part of metropolitan UK.
- [GOV.UK, Living in Gibraltar](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/living-in-gibraltar): Gibraltar is a British Overseas Territory.
- [French Guiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Guiana): overseas department/region with the same departmental status as metropolitan departments; integral France. The pinned France/Brazil border sources explicitly include the two Guiana neighbours.
- [Mediterranean Sea, pinned 1384519403](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?oldid=1384519403): coastal states and the separate Gibraltar/base exceptions. Applying the stated territory rule excludes UK, not France.
- [List of island countries, pinned 1374131242](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?oldid=1374131242): explicitly excludes continental Australia; 50 island countries, including the two freely associated states and two partially recognised states, excludes dependencies.
- [Left- and right-hand traffic, pinned 1384515038](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?oldid=1384515038): jurisdiction table distinguishes mainland China/US from Hong Kong/Macau/US Virgin Islands.

There is no active Arctic, Antarctic-territory, Caribbean/Pacific/Atlantic-coastline country predicate beyond the explicitly reviewed treaty, time-zone and Mediterranean questions.
