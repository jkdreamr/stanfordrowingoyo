# Final constrained replacement batch

33 prompts: books 1, everyday 2, film 5, geography 6, history 3, nature 5,
science 6 and sports 5. Frozen guesses preceded source inspection; selected
guesses preserve their exact strings and order. Rejected candidates remain in
the raw artifact. Exact normalized-key recall is a model brainstorm measurement,
not representative player telemetry or a proof of completeness.

Every set has a pinned Wikipedia revision, reviewed members and exact counts in
`constrained.json`. The source set is complete within each explicit scope.
Android uses 29 public/internal dessert names, merging Snow Cone v2 into
Snow Cone. Mac releases are the nine cat codenames; Star Wars excludes television-first
Ewok films; Wizarding World excludes books, games and the stage play; DreamWorks
includes Aardman co-productions and the 2025 live-action Dragon remake, but not
streaming-only Orion or direct-to-video Joseph. The two Dragon releases retain
separate year-qualified identities. Wes Anderson includes the feature-length
Henry Sugar anthology, accepting its familiar shortened title.

Geography uses current national/UN population estimates, both Bass and Messner
Seven Summits lists, the 19 directly represented G20 countries, and France's 13
top-level overseas administrative units. Tahiti is a common metonym alias for
French Polynesia, not a fourteenth territory. Monarchies cover UN members and
the Vatican/Holy See observer, not dependent or freely associated nonmember
territories. Original Antarctic signatories include USSR/Russia continuity.

Nature uses the CFA breed roll (including noncompetitive classes and division
aliases), living Vulpes, the pinned full cetacean Delphinidae taxonomy, and the
Mammal Diversity Database-based cingulate list. Historical taxonomic names and
subspecies alias their represented current species. Hurricanes must have an
official assigned name, rather than retrospective labels for unnamed storms.

Science includes every joint award recipient and the 24 Apollo lunar travelers,
not only walkers. Discovery missions must actually have launched. Sports include
all 76 NBA anniversary selections (the vote tied), the twelve current men's
Test teams, and both Paris 2024 sports and disciplines, not individual events.
F1 constructor titles are chassis-constructor wins; host countries exclude
canceled venues and namesakes such as San Marino. West Germany aliases Germany.

The 2023 UN population candidate was replaced by the current population table.
Berkeley's discovery claim did not enumerate a defensible complete element set;
it was dropped. The complete 235-person Medicine set was replaced for budget by
the ten Soviet probes that returned Venus surface data; the pre-source frozen
Venus backup brainstorm is transferred verbatim into its final ID. American
Pioneer Venus is outside this explicitly Soviet scope. NATO codewords were
dropped as an exact duplicate of existing nato-alphabet; Android replaces them.
AKC terriers and ratites remain unused backups. The frozen Pixar brainstorm is
reserved for the separately requested duplicate-retirement replacement.
The source text/data retains Wikipedia CC BY-SA revision provenance; existing
ENABLE and wordfreq inputs are unchanged. No matcher, UI or input-cap changes
were made in this batch. Visual behavior was not checked.
